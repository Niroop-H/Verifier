Platform capabilities

Verification tools, connected by an evidence-first workflow.

Explore the supported stages of AEGIS EDA Verifier. Tool execution depends on installed dependencies, valid design inputs and compatible project collateral.

01

RTL lint and compile

Use supported Verilator and Icarus Verilog adapters to detect selected syntax, elaboration and lint issues. Language coverage depends on the selected tool and flags.

Status: local adapters and regression fixtures documented
02

RTL simulation

Run testbenches with Icarus Verilog and VVP. A meaningful result depends on testbench quality, assertions and coverage—not just a successful process exit.

Status: local adapter and fixtures documented
03

Formal property checking

Execute configured SymbiYosys tasks with Z3 where supported. Reports distinguish counterexamples, bounded checks and proof outcomes.

Status: local adapter and fixtures documented
04

Synthesis

Use Yosys for hierarchy checks, process lowering, optimization and structural statistics. Synthesis reports are not equivalent to physical sign-off.

Status: local adapter and fixtures documented
05

Timing and physical checks

OpenSTA timing and KLayout DRC require appropriate netlists, libraries, constraints, technology data and rule decks. Unsupported or missing inputs must block a check.

Status: limited, input-dependent workflows
06

VHDL analysis

GHDL can analyze supported VHDL inputs. Functional verification still requires a suitable testbench and checks.

Status: local analysis adapter documented

One report. Clear boundaries.

Every result should say which tool ran, what inputs were used and what the outcome does—and does not—establish.

PASS

The named check executed and met its defined success criteria.

FAIL

The check executed and reported a failure or counterexample.

BLOCKED / NOT RUN

Required tools, inputs, constraints or execution services were unavailable or the check was not requested.
