# Third-party tool licensing

MAH Quantum Verify invokes external EDA executables and does not relicense them. Users are responsible for reviewing the exact release and license of each installed tool and of any PDK/rule deck.

Tools commonly used by this project have different licenses and distribution requirements. Preserve upstream copyright notices and license texts when redistributing tool source or binaries. Do not bundle licensed commercial tools, customer data or restricted foundry files in this repository. This notice is informational and is not a substitute for a per-release legal review.
